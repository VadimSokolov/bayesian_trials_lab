## What it is
RBesT is an R package of Bayesian evidence synthesis tools. [sec-software-tools] It builds meta-analytic-predictive priors, robustifies them, and gives operating characteristics for designs that use historical data. [sec-software-tools]

## Why it matters
The book marks it as relevant to the borrowing chapter. [sec-software-tools]

## In the book
Of the tools listed, only rpact, gsDesign, BOIN, brms and rstan were version-checked on the build machine, so RBesT was not checked. [sec-software-tools]
