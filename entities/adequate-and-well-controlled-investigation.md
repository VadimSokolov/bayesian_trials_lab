## What it is
The statute leaves the phrase undefined, and FDA's regulation at 21 CFR 314.126 defines it. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means] Its purpose is to distinguish the effect of the drug from other influences, such as spontaneous change in the disease course, placebo effect, or biased observation. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]

The book paraphrases seven characteristics. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]
- A clear statement of objectives and of the method of analysis. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]
- A design that permits a valid comparison with a control. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]
- Subjects selected by an adequate method. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]
- Assignment that minimizes bias and assures comparability at baseline, usually by randomization. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]
- Measures against bias by subjects, observers and analysts, usually blinding. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]
- Well-defined and reliable methods of assessing response. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]
- An adequate analysis of the results. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]

## Why it matters
The book calls this the most useful list for a trialist to carry. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means] The regulation expects the protocol to state the proposed analysis methods, which is the regulatory root of pre-specification, for Bayesian analyses as for frequentist ones. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]

## In the book
The regulation names placebo concurrent control, dose-comparison, no treatment, active treatment and historical control as recognized control types. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means] The 2026 draft cautions that external controls differ from the treated group "because of the lack of randomized treatment assignment". [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]

## Check yourself
- Q: Which item of the list is the root of pre-specification? A: Item 1, a clear statement of objectives and of the proposed methods of analysis in the protocol, together with item 7, an adequate analysis. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]
- Q: Is historical control an admitted control type? A: Yes, but the 2026 draft warns about its weakness. [3/the-regulation-what-adequate-and-well-controlled-means]
