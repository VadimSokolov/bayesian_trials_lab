## What it is
Study 201 of lecanemab in early Alzheimer's disease was a Bayesian phase 2b trial with five dose regimens and placebo, response-adaptive allocation, and interim analyses starting after 196 patients and then every 50. [17/lecanemab-bayesian-dose-finding-in-phase-2b]

## In the book
It identified 10 mg/kg biweekly as the estimated 90 percent effective dose, the dose achieving 90 percent of the modeled maximum effect. [17/lecanemab-bayesian-dose-finding-in-phase-2b] The trial did not meet its prespecified 12-month primary criterion: the posterior probability of at least a 25 percent reduction in decline was 0.64, short of the required 0.80. [17/lecanemab-bayesian-dose-finding-in-phase-2b] The selected regimen was carried into the phase 3 program. [17/lecanemab-bayesian-dose-finding-in-phase-2b]

## Why it matters
A design can fail its primary criterion and still produce the right dose, so the protocol should say in advance how dose-selection evidence will be read in that event. [17/lecanemab-bayesian-dose-finding-in-phase-2b] A prespecified Bayesian criterion that is not met is a result, to be reported as one. [17/lecanemab-bayesian-dose-finding-in-phase-2b]
