## What it is
SampleSizeProportions and SampleSizeMeans are R packages by Joseph and Bélisle. [sec-software-tools] They give Bayesian sample size criteria (ALC, ACC, WOC) for proportions and means. [sec-software-tools]

## Why it matters
They are the book's reference for Bayesian sample size criteria. [sec-software-tools]

## In the book
The lupus example in the power chapter uses SampleSizeProportions 1.1.3. [sec-software-tools]

## Pitfalls
The descriptions in the book cover only the purpose of each tool, so a reader should consult the documentation for current features and versions. [sec-software-tools]

## Check yourself
- Q: Which three criteria do they implement? A: ALC, ACC and WOC. [sec-software-tools]
- Q: Which version does the lupus example use? A: SampleSizeProportions 1.1.3. [sec-software-tools]
