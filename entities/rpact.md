## What it is
rpact is an R package for confirmatory adaptive design. [sec-software-tools] It covers group sequential designs, sample size reassessment and combination tests, with simulation. [sec-software-tools]

## Why it matters
It is one of the established trial-design tools the book lists as in wide use. [sec-software-tools] The descriptions give only the purpose of each tool. [sec-software-tools]
