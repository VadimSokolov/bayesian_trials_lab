## What it is
AWARD-5 tested dulaglutide in type 2 diabetes. It started in 2008 with seven doses and two controls. [17/dulaglutide-award-5-seamless-dose-finding]

## In the book
Stage 1 used response-adaptive randomization with interim analyses every two weeks, scored by a composite clinical utility index of HbA1c, weight, heart rate and blood pressure. [17/dulaglutide-award-5-seamless-dose-finding] Longitudinal modeling predicted the 12-month outcome from early data. [17/dulaglutide-award-5-seamless-dose-finding] The algorithm selected two non-adjacent doses, 0.75 and 1.50 mg, at the earliest permitted time, and stage 2 used fixed randomization against controls. [17/dulaglutide-award-5-seamless-dose-finding] In April 2009 randomization to 3.0 mg was stopped on the monitoring board's recommendation after heart rate increases and pancreatic safety concerns. [17/dulaglutide-award-5-seamless-dose-finding] Both doses were approved by FDA in 2014. By Berry's estimate the design saved at least a year. [17/dulaglutide-award-5-seamless-dose-finding]

## Why it matters
It needs a prespecified algorithm, an agreed role for the data monitoring committee, which could depart from the algorithm only on safety, and a simulated operating-characteristics report. [17/dulaglutide-award-5-seamless-dose-finding]
