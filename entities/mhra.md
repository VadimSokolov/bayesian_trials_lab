## What it is
The MHRA is the UK medicines regulator. It applies the same statistical principles as EMA, since both adopt ICH guidelines. [sec-regulatory-eu] Its document in the book is the draft guideline on the use of external control arms based on real-world data to support regulatory decisions. [sec-regulatory-eu]

## Why it matters
External controls are a form of borrowing. [sec-regulatory-eu] The draft parallels FDA's February 2023 draft guidance on externally controlled trials. [sec-regulatory-eu] The three regulators share pre-specification, control of false positive conclusions, simulation and integrity. [sec-regulatory-eu]

## In the book
The MHRA consulted on the draft in 2025. [sec-regulatory-eu] The document table lists it as Draft, 2025, relied on in the borrowing chapter. [sec-regulatory-table]

## Pitfalls
It is a draft, so its status can change, and the book gives the status as of November 2026. [sec-regulatory-table]
