## What it is
The 21st Century Cures Act of 2016 directed FDA to promote novel trial designs, including complex adaptive and Bayesian designs. [3/the-complex-innovative-trial-design-cid-paired-meeting-program] FDA's response was the CID paired meeting program, described by the final guidance of December 2020. [3/the-complex-innovative-trial-design-cid-paired-meeting-program] A sponsor applies with a description of the design, and accepted sponsors receive a pair of meetings, the second after the simulation work discussed in the first. [3/the-complex-innovative-trial-design-cid-paired-meeting-program]

## Why it matters
It is the main route for a sponsor proposing a Bayesian or otherwise complex adaptive design. [3/the-complex-innovative-trial-design-cid-paired-meeting-program] Admission is selective, and the meetings do not replace the ordinary milestones. [3/the-complex-innovative-trial-design-cid-paired-meeting-program] Use the CID program for the trial's decision rules and the MIDD program for the models that feed them. [3/the-midd-paired-meeting-program]

## In the book
A CID package normally includes the proposed design, the scenarios, the operating characteristics and a comparison with a simpler alternative. [3/the-complex-innovative-trial-design-cid-paired-meeting-program] Two case studies come through the program: a pediatric multiple sclerosis non-inferiority study and an EMAS epilepsy study. [sec-regulatory-practice] The program is active, a PDUFA commitment. [sec-documents-named]
