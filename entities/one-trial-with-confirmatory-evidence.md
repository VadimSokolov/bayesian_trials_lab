## What it is
The Food and Drug Administration Modernization Act of 1997 added a sentence to section 505(d). [3/the-1997-amendment-one-trial-and-confirmatory-evidence] If the Secretary determines, based on relevant science, that data from one adequate and well-controlled clinical investigation and confirmatory evidence are sufficient, those data may be treated as substantial evidence. [3/the-1997-amendment-one-trial-and-confirmatory-evidence] The confirmatory evidence may come from before or after the trial. [3/the-1997-amendment-one-trial-and-confirmatory-evidence]

## Why it matters
The power is discretionary: it is FDA's determination, not a right of the sponsor. [3/the-1997-amendment-one-trial-and-confirmatory-evidence] It has been used more often over time, especially in oncology, rare diseases and pediatric settings where a second trial is impractical or unethical. [3/the-1997-amendment-one-trial-and-confirmatory-evidence] The permission is of the form "if FDA agrees", so the route goes through a meeting. [sec-regulatory-force]

## In the book
Plans for one trial should be discussed with FDA before the trial starts. [3/the-2026-substantial-evidence-draft] REBYOTA was approved on one phase 3 trial plus a Bayesian model that borrowed from a phase 2b study. [3/rebyota-one-trial-standing-in-for-two] Its observed posterior probability of superiority was 0.991, which clears the 0.9750338 threshold for one adequate and well-controlled trial and misses the 0.9993275 bar for one trial standing in for two, so the evidence counts as one adequate and well-controlled trial, not two, and approval rested on the totality of evidence. [3/rebyota-one-trial-standing-in-for-two]

The arithmetic behind the standard: two independent trials each at one-sided 0.025 pass a useless drug with probability $0.025 \times 0.025 = 1/1600$, while a single trial at 0.025 passes it 1 time in 40. [3/what-the-standard-implies-for-the-numbers]

## Pitfalls
The thresholds in REBYOTA were case-specific evidentiary criteria agreed with FDA, not numerical standards set by the 1997 amendment. [3/rebyota-one-trial-standing-in-for-two] An informative Bayesian prior is a close cousin of "one trial plus confirmatory evidence", but FDA still evaluates its relevance, weight and contribution. [3/the-2026-substantial-evidence-draft]
