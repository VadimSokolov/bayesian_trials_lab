## What it is
brms is an R package that fits multilevel models through Stan with a formula interface. [sec-software-tools]

## Why it matters
It is used to fit analysis models when conjugate formulas are not available. [sec-software-tools]

## In the book
The build machine had brms 2.23.0. [sec-software-tools]
