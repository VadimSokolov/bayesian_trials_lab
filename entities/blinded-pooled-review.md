## What it is

Blinded pooled review of the whole trial asks whether the overall event rate is higher than expected, without revealing the arms. [sec-safety-aggregate]

## Why it matters

It lets a sponsor watch safety while the trial stays blinded. The FDA guidance recommends keeping individual treatment assignments blinded where the trial is blinded, with unblinded aggregate review by a group independent of the sponsor's study team. [sec-safety]

## In the book

Given credible external information about adverse event rates in comparable untreated patients, Gould and Wang describe Bayesian monitoring for potential excess risk using pooled blinded data. Their title calls it the canary in the coal mine. [sec-safety-aggregate] It sits beside the unblinded DMC review, which compares arms. [sec-safety-aggregate]

## Pitfalls

- It needs credible external rates for the comparison to mean anything. [sec-safety-aggregate]

## Check yourself

- Q: What does the pooled blinded review compare the overall rate with? A: The expected rate from external information on comparable untreated patients. [sec-safety-aggregate]
