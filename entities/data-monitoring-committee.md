## What it is

A DMC (older terms DSMB and IDMC) is a small independent group, typically three to seven members, with clinicians in the therapeutic area and at least one statistician. It reviews accumulating data and recommends to the sponsor whether to continue, modify or stop. [11/what-a-dmc-does]

FDA's 2006 guidance covers it, and a February 2024 draft revision is not final. [11/what-a-dmc-does]

## Why it matters

The DMC sees unblinded comparative data and the sponsor does not. An independent statistical group prepares the closed reports. Access by people who can influence the trial's conduct can make a confirmatory result uninterpretable. [11/what-a-dmc-does]

## In the book

The charter, fixed before the first patient, sets membership, meeting schedule, open and closed report content, stopping guidelines and the path to the sponsor. [11/what-a-dmc-does]

Boundaries are guidelines. A DMC may recommend stopping for harm without a boundary crossed. Discretion does not by itself inflate the efficacy error, as long as a claim of success stays restricted to crossings of the prespecified boundary. [11/what-a-dmc-does]

The sponsor learns only the recommendation. A recommendation to stop discloses much, and one to modify can disclose a great deal. [11/what-a-dmc-does]

The book's reporting template has the committee shown the posterior probabilities of benefit and harm and the predictive probability of success, each next to its boundary, and allows it to decline to stop at a crossed boundary and explain why. [11/a-reporting-template-for-the-dmc]

## Pitfalls

- What can damage the guarantee is a decision that uses comparative data outside the rule, and conduct that compromises integrity. [11/what-a-dmc-does]
- Early stops overestimate the effect. [11/what-a-dmc-does]

## Check yourself

- Q: Who sees unblinded interim comparisons? A: The DMC and an independent statistical group, not the sponsor or investigators. [11/what-a-dmc-does]
- Q: Can a DMC decline to stop at a crossed efficacy boundary? A: Yes, and the type I error stays at most alpha if success claims need a prespecified crossing. [11/what-a-dmc-does]
