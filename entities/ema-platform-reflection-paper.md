## What it is
The EMA draft Reflection Paper on Methodological Aspects in Confirmatory Platform Trials (EMA/217509/2026) was in public consultation from 19 October 2026 to 31 January 2027. [B/documents] It covers pre-specification, randomization, controls including non-concurrent controls, type I error control and estimands for platform trials. [20/ema]

## Why it matters
The draft is more cautious than FDA about the shared control of a platform. [20/ema] It notes that a shared control makes errors correlated, and that the chance of at least one false positive may fall while the chance of several rises. [20/ema]

## In the book
Tightening each arm's level to hold down the chance of several false positives would need thresholds stricter than Bonferroni, so the draft offers alternatives such as larger control groups. [20/ema] It treats the choice of family as a design decision to be made in advance and argued. [20/ema] It puts more weight than FDA on arms that share a submission or a sponsor, and on the distribution of the number of errors. [20/ema]

## Pitfalls
For a program whose arms are all developed by one sponsor for one eventual label, a regulator in either jurisdiction may regard the arms as a single family, which needs a conversation. [20/ema]

## Check yourself
- Q: What does a shared control do to errors across arms, according to the draft? A: It makes them correlated. [20/ema]
- Q: Is the choice of family a post hoc matter? A: No, it is a design decision to be made in advance and argued. [20/ema]
