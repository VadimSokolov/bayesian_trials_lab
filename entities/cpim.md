## What it is
CPIM is a nonbinding, informal discussion of methods or technologies not tied to a single product or an active application. [3/critical-path-innovation-meetings-cpim] FDA's stated aim is to respond to a request within 14 days. [3/critical-path-innovation-meetings-cpim]

## Why it matters
For a group developing a general methodology, such as a class of Bayesian borrowing models, it is a low-cost way to get an informal view early. [3/critical-path-innovation-meetings-cpim]

## In the book
The meeting chooser lists CPIM for a general method that is not tied to a product, and marks it as not binding. [3/how-to-choose-the-meeting] The program is active and the discussions are product-independent. [sec-documents]

## Pitfalls
Because it is not tied to a product or application, it cannot give agreement on a specific design. [3/critical-path-innovation-meetings-cpim]

## Check yourself
- Q: Can a CPIM be about one sponsor's pending application? A: No, it covers methods not tied to a single product or active application. [3/critical-path-innovation-meetings-cpim]
- Q: Is it binding? A: No. [3/critical-path-innovation-meetings-cpim]
