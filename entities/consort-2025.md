## What it is
CONSORT 2025 is the reporting guideline for the results of a randomized trial. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
The adaptive-designs extension, ACE, extends CONSORT 2010 and predates CONSORT 2025. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## Why it matters
It is one of several guidelines around the protocol, and none is a regulatory requirement for a Bayesian trial. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
It is written for the generic randomized trial, so a Bayesian trial must add its own items. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## In the book
The items a Bayesian trial adds are the same ones the chapter has put in the protocol: the prior and its source, the success criterion with its threshold and calibration, the interim schedule and the simulation report, the software and seeds, and the sensitivity analyses to the prior. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
A report that gives each of them where the generic checklist asks for its traditional counterpart satisfies the intent of all of these lists. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
SPIRIT 2025 is the corresponding checklist for the protocol. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## Pitfalls
Only a few guidelines are written for Bayesian analyses, and they are author guidelines. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## Check yourself
- Q: Which extension covers adaptive trials, and which CONSORT does it extend? A: ACE, which extends CONSORT 2010. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
- Q: What must a Bayesian report add to CONSORT items? A: The prior, success criterion with calibration, schedule and simulation report, software and seeds, and prior sensitivity. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
