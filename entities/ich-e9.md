## What it is
E9, Statistical Principles for Clinical Trials, reached Step 4 on 5 February 1998. [sec-regulatory-ich] It describes randomization, blinding, the analysis populations, the primary variable, interim analysis and multiplicity. [sec-regulatory-ich]

## Why it matters
After more than 25 years E9 remains the foundation. [sec-regulatory-ich] It says that all interim analyses should be carefully planned in advance and described in the protocol, and that group sequential designs are the usual way to look repeatedly. [sec-regulatory-ich] On multiplicity it says the usual frequentist approach may necessitate an adjustment to the type I error. [sec-regulatory-ich]

## In the book
E9 mentions Bayesian approaches only briefly, in Section 1.2 and the Glossary, and does not elaborate them, so it neither prohibits nor privileges them. [sec-regulatory-ich] The document list gives it as Final (Step 4), 5 February 1998, covering randomization, blinding, the intention-to-treat principle, multiplicity and interim analysis.
