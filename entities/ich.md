## What it is
The International Council for Harmonisation of Technical Requirements for Pharmaceuticals for Human Use brings regulators and industry together to agree guidelines. [sec-regulatory-ich] A guideline reaches Step 4 when the ICH Assembly adopts the final text, and regional regulators then implement it. [sec-regulatory-ich] Step 2 is a draft out for consultation. [sec-regulatory-ich]

## Why it matters
FDA publishes an ICH guideline as guidance and EMA as a scientific guideline. [sec-regulatory-ich] Six documents shape the book: E8(R1), E9, E9(R1), E20, E11A and M15. [sec-regulatory-ich]

## In the book
- E8(R1), Step 4, 6 October 2021, brings in quality by design. [sec-regulatory-ich]
- E9, Step 4, 5 February 1998, is the foundation, and it neither prohibits nor privileges Bayesian approaches. [sec-regulatory-ich]
- E20 is a draft at Step 2, endorsed 25 June 2025, and the work plan targets sign-off in February 2027. [sec-regulatory-ich]
- E11A, Step 4, August 2024, covers pediatric extrapolation. [sec-regulatory-ich]
- M15, Step 4, January 2026, covers model-informed drug development. [sec-regulatory-ich]

## Pitfalls
Until E20 is final it binds no region. [sec-regulatory-ich]

## Check yourself
- Q: What does Step 4 mean? A: The ICH Assembly adopts the final text. [sec-regulatory-ich]
- Q: Does E9 elaborate Bayesian approaches? A: No, it mentions them only briefly. [sec-regulatory-ich]
