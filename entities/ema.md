## What it is
EMA and the UK MHRA apply the same statistical principles as FDA, since both adopt ICH guidelines. [sec-regulatory-eu] Their documents on adaptive and complex designs differ in emphasis and date. [sec-regulatory-eu]

## Why it matters
A sponsor seeking approval in several regions needs to read them together. [sec-regulatory-eu] The EMA drafts are the more cautious on flexibility in the primary analysis of a confirmatory trial. [sec-regulatory-eu] A global program should plan European scientific advice in parallel with the US meetings. [sec-regulatory-eu]

## In the book
- The adaptive-design reflection paper of October 2007 (CHMP/EWP/2459/02) is more conservative than the 2019 FDA guidance and still current until E20 is final in the EU. [sec-regulatory-eu]
- The Q&A on complex clinical trials (EMA/298712/2022, May 2022) covers master protocols, platform designs and Bayesian methods. [sec-regulatory-eu]
- A concept paper on Bayesian methods (EMA/CHMP/1813/2026) was open for consultation from 30 January to 30 April 2026, with a draft reflection paper tentatively set for September 2027 and a final text for June 2028. [sec-regulatory-eu]
- The draft platform-trial reflection paper (EMA/217509/2026) is in consultation from 19 October 2026 to 31 January 2027. [sec-regulatory-eu]

## Pitfalls
The platform draft says "response adaptive randomisation (RAR) is not supported" and that non-concurrent controls are usually not acceptable for the primary analysis, so design to the stricter position until it is final. [sec-regulatory-eu]

## Check yourself
- Q: Does the EMA platform draft discuss Bayesian methods? A: No. [sec-regulatory-eu]
- Q: Is a concept paper guidance? A: No, it proposes that a reflection paper be written. [sec-regulatory-eu]
