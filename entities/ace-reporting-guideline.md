## What it is
The CONSORT 2010 adaptive-designs extension (ACE) is the reporting counterpart for adaptive trials. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
It extends CONSORT 2010, predates CONSORT 2025, and adds items on the prespecified adaptations. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## Why it matters
A Bayesian adaptive design is reported in two layers: the generic randomized-trial items and the adaptation items ACE supplies. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
The guideline is not a regulatory requirement for a Bayesian trial. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## In the book
The book lists ACE beside SPIRIT 2013, CONSORT 2025, a guideline on the content of statistical analysis plans, and the Bayesian checklists. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
The Bayesian items that these lists do not name are the prior, calibrated success criterion, interim schedule, simulation report, software with seeds, and prior sensitivity. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
Placing each where the generic checklist asks for its traditional counterpart satisfies the intent of all the lists. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## Pitfalls
None of the reporting guidelines around the protocol is a regulatory requirement for a Bayesian trial. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
