## What it is
Patent expiry is fixed, so a cardiovascular indication reached earlier adds the same number of years of protected, label-driven sales. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
The estimate multiplies sales by an assumed label share and by the years brought forward. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]

## Why it matters
It is the largest sum in the accounting and rests on the least. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]

## In the book
Novo Nordisk reported global Wegovy sales of DKK 58.2 billion for 2024. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
The label share is not public, so the book assumes 10 to 25 percent, and converts at an assumed 6.9 DKK per dollar. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
For REWIND, Trulicity revenue in 2019 and the FDA approval of its cardiovascular indication on 21 February 2020 anchor the analogue, with an assumed 10 to 20 percent label share. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
The REWIND analogue assumes 24 months of linear enrollment and 85 months between registered start and primary completion. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
In REWIND the all-trial median saving is zero, because fewer than half of the simulated trials cross, so the revenue figures hold only conditionally on the rule acting. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
For the SELECT reconstruction, 2024 sales of about $8.4 billion, brought forward by the all-trial median saving of 2.2 years, give $1.8 to $4.6 billion. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
For REWIND, conditional on the rule acting, the decision comes at 680 of 1,257 events (month 51, 2.8 years early), giving $1.2 to $2.3 billion. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
The key points call the SELECT figure of order a few billion dollars of Wegovy revenue, an upper bound and not profit. [21/key-points]

## Pitfalls
Approval, reimbursement and uptake would not all have moved forward by the full statistical acceleration. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
The sales level, the label share and the exchange rate are assumptions. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
Multiplying a crossing probability by a conditional median would not give an expected revenue. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]

## Check yourself
- Q: Is the figure profit? A: No, it is revenue, and an upper bound. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
- Q: Why is the REWIND saving only conditional? A: The Bayesian rule acts in fewer than half the simulated trials. [21/label-revenue-brought-forward]
