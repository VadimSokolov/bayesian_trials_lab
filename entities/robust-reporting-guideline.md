## What it is
ROBUST is a seven-item checklist for reporting a Bayesian analysis of a clinical study. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
It is one of only a few guidelines written for Bayesian analyses, and they are author guidelines. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## Why it matters
Generic checklists such as SPIRIT and CONSORT do not name the Bayesian components of a trial. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
A Bayesian trial adds the prior and its source, the success criterion with its threshold and calibration, the interim schedule and the simulation report, the software and seeds, and the sensitivity analyses to the prior. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## In the book
ROBUST sits with BARG and a consensus checklist for clinicians who read a trial analyzed by Bayesian methods. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
The book's own sponsor and reviewer checklists carry the same content in operational form, from the estimand to the meeting plan. [23/checklists]

## Pitfalls
None of the reporting guidelines around the protocol is a regulatory requirement for a Bayesian trial. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
