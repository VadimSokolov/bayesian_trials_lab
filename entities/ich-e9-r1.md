## What it is
E9(R1) is the Addendum on Estimands and Sensitivity Analysis, Step 4, 20 November 2019. [sec-regulatory-ich] FDA labels its own publication of the document May 2021. [sec-regulatory-ich] It defines an estimand as "a precise description of the treatment effect reflecting the clinical question posed by a given clinical trial objective". [sec-regulatory-ich]

## Why it matters
It requires that "the targets of estimation are to be defined in advance of a clinical trial". [sec-regulatory-ich] It introduces intercurrent events, events after treatment initiation that affect the interpretation or existence of the measurements. [sec-regulatory-ich] A posterior probability is only as interpretable as the estimand it is posterior about. [sec-regulatory-ich]

## In the book
The pivotal protocol fixes the primary endpoint, the estimand with a strategy for intercurrent events such as rescue medication or discontinuation, and the primary analysis. [sec-regulatory-traditional] The five strategies are treatment policy, hypothetical, composite variable, while on treatment and principal stratum. [sec-regulatory-ich]

## Check yourself
- Q: How many strategies does E9(R1) name for intercurrent events? A: Five. [sec-regulatory-ich]
- Q: When must estimation targets be defined? A: In advance of the trial. [sec-regulatory-ich]
