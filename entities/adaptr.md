## What it is
adaptr is an R package for simulating Bayesian adaptive multi-arm trials. [sec-software-tools] It handles response-adaptive randomization, early stopping and calibration of the stopping rules, summarized as operating characteristics. [sec-software-tools]

## Why it matters
The book calls it the closest open R counterpart to the simulation part of `bayestrials` for multi-arm designs. [sec-software-tools]

## In the book
The book lists it among the R packages on CRAN. [sec-software-tools]

## Check yourself
- Q: What does adaptr summarize its simulations as? A: Operating characteristics. [sec-software-tools]
- Q: Which part of bayestrials is it closest to? A: The simulation part, for multi-arm designs. [sec-software-tools]
