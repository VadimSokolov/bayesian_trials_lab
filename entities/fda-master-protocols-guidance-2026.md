## What it is
A first draft appeared in December 2023, and a revised draft that "revises and replaces" it was announced in the Federal Register on 24 June 2026 (91 FR 37991), with comments closing on 24 August 2026. [3/master-protocols-for-drug-and-biological-product-development-revised-draft-june-2026] It is issued under the Food and Drug Omnibus Reform Act of 2022. [3/master-protocols-for-drug-and-biological-product-development-revised-draft-june-2026] It defines a master protocol as a protocol with multiple substudies, which may have different objectives. [3/master-protocols-for-drug-and-biological-product-development-revised-draft-june-2026]

## Why it matters
- The primary comparison should use "only concurrently eligible control participants". [3/master-protocols-for-drug-and-biological-product-development-revised-draft-june-2026]
- Nonconcurrent controls are allowed only in rare circumstances agreed with FDA in advance, with concurrent-only sensitivity analyses. [3/master-protocols-for-drug-and-biological-product-development-revised-draft-june-2026]
- FDA generally does not recommend multiplicity adjustment across different drugs, but expects adjustment across doses or formulations of the same drug. [3/master-protocols-for-drug-and-biological-product-development-revised-draft-june-2026]
- A master protocol pre-IND meeting is requested as Type B. [3/master-protocols-for-drug-and-biological-product-development-revised-draft-june-2026]

## In the book
The EMA draft on platform trials is stricter on controls and allocation, so a global platform should treat the stricter position as the design constraint. [sec-regulatory-eu] The guidance complements the March 2022 oncology master protocols guidance. [3/master-protocols-for-drug-and-biological-product-development-revised-draft-june-2026]

## Check yourself
- Q: Does FDA recommend adjusting across drugs in a master protocol? A: Generally not, because the expected number of type I errors is the same as in independent trials. [3/master-protocols-for-drug-and-biological-product-development-revised-draft-june-2026]
- Q: Which meeting type fits a master protocol pre-IND? A: Type B. [3/master-protocols-for-drug-and-biological-product-development-revised-draft-june-2026]
