## What it is

PANORAMIC took the pragmatic platform approach into UK primary care. It analyzed its primary outcome with Bayesian logistic regression using weakly informative Cauchy priors. [20/pragmatic-pandemic-platforms-recovery-and-panoramic]

## Why it matters

Not every pandemic platform was an elaborate Bayesian adaptive design. RECOVERY was a large, pragmatic, open-label platform analyzed with frequentist methods, and PANORAMIC shows that simplicity and volume can do the work of a more elaborate adaptive design. [20/pragmatic-pandemic-platforms-recovery-and-panoramic]

## In the book

The molnupiravir comparison randomized 25,783 participants between December 2021 and April 2022 to molnupiravir plus usual care or usual care alone. The platform's total of 26,411 includes other comparisons. Hospitalization or death within 28 days was about 1% in each group (adjusted odds ratio 1.06), a clear negative result. [20/pragmatic-pandemic-platforms-recovery-and-panoramic] A platform that can enroll that many community patients in under five months shows that its value comes first from infrastructure and speed. [20/pragmatic-pandemic-platforms-recovery-and-panoramic] The book contrasts it with RECOVERY, whose first major result was a dexamethasone rate ratio of 0.83 for 28-day mortality. [20/pragmatic-pandemic-platforms-recovery-and-panoramic]

## Pitfalls

The book groups it with RECOVERY as evidence that a platform's value comes first from infrastructure and speed, not from adaptive machinery. [20/pragmatic-pandemic-platforms-recovery-and-panoramic]
