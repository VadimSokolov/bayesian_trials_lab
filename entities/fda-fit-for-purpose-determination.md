## What it is
The FDA Drug Development Tools Fit-for-Purpose Initiative lists dose-finding methods FDA has reviewed for a stated context of use. [sec-dose] The list as printed, current as of 10 January 2025, includes BOIN (determination issued 10 December 2021) and MCP-Mod (26 May 2016). [sec-dose]

## Why it matters
BOIN is the one model-assisted design with an FDA determination. What was reviewed was the method for a stated context of use, phase 1 dose finding with a toxicity endpoint. [17/boin-and-fdas-fit-for-purpose-determination]

## Pitfalls
The regulator's acceptance attaches to a method and a context of use. A sponsor still has to supply its own simulations and a protocol-specific justification. [17/boin-and-fdas-fit-for-purpose-determination]

## Check yourself
- Q: Does a fit-for-purpose determination exempt a sponsor from simulations? A: No. [17/boin-and-fdas-fit-for-purpose-determination]
- Q: Which determination is older, BOIN or MCP-Mod? A: MCP-Mod, 26 May 2016, against 10 December 2021. [sec-dose]
