## What it is

A conventional program builds a trial, answers one question and takes it down. A master protocol keeps the site contracts, database, randomization system, monitoring board and regulatory file standing and lets questions pass through. [sec-platform] FDA's June 2026 draft defines it as "a protocol designed with multiple substudies, which may have different objectives and involve coordinated efforts to evaluate one or more drugs". [20/definitions-master-protocol-basket-umbrella-platform]

## Why it matters

The idea is not Bayesian. The frequentist STAMPEDE opened in 2005 and shows that a shared control and a standing infrastructure pay for themselves. [sec-platform] The master protocol holds what is common: eligibility, endpoints, schedule of assessments, randomization system, statistical framework and governance. [20/definitions-master-protocol-basket-umbrella-platform]

## In the book

The treatment-specific part sits in a module, called an intervention-specific appendix, sub-protocol, substudy appendix or, in REMAP-CAP, a domain-specific appendix. It holds the dose, safety monitoring, exclusions and decision thresholds. [20/definitions-master-protocol-basket-umbrella-platform] The terms and taxonomy were set out by Woodcock and LaVange (2017). [20/definitions-master-protocol-basket-umbrella-platform] The three kinds are umbrella, basket and platform. [20/definitions-master-protocol-basket-umbrella-platform]

## Pitfalls

A master protocol supports a marketing application for each drug on the evidence of that drug's comparison, and the legal standard of substantial evidence is not lowered. [20/what-changes-in-the-protocol] The FDA 2026 document is a draft that recommends and does not bind. [sec-platform]

## Check yourself

- Q: What belongs in the master document and what in the module? A: Common items in the master, drug-specific dose, safety and thresholds in the module. [20/definitions-master-protocol-basket-umbrella-platform]
- Q: Is a master protocol necessarily Bayesian? A: No. [sec-platform]
