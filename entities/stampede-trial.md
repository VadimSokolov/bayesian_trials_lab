## What it is

STAMPEDE (Systemic Therapy in Advancing or Metastatic Prostate Cancer: Evaluation of Drug Efficacy) was designed at the MRC Clinical Trials Unit at UCL as a multi-arm multi-stage trial. The first patient was randomized in October 2005 to one of six arms, a standard hormone therapy control and five research arms, with allocation 2:1:1:1:1:1. [20/the-frequentist-platform-multi-arm-multi-stage-designs]

## Why it matters

It is the standing reminder that Bayesian machinery is optional for a platform: a common control, pre-specified stopping rules, intermediate outcomes and standing infrastructure answered several questions in the time that separate trials would need. [20/the-frequentist-platform-multi-arm-multi-stage-designs]

## In the book

Early stages use failure-free survival as an intermediate outcome, ahead of the definitive outcome, overall survival. [20/the-frequentist-platform-multi-arm-multi-stage-designs] Its strategy was comparison-level testing, and its investigators report a familywise error rate of 6.75% for the three original comparisons reaching their targets. [20/the-frequentist-platform-multi-arm-multi-stage-designs] When the standard of care changed, the control regimen changed with it. [20/the-frequentist-platform-multi-arm-multi-stage-designs] The abiraterone comparison joined later and, in men starting long-term hormone therapy, reduced the hazard of death (hazard ratio 0.63). The correlation between its test statistic and those of the original arms was 0.12. [20/the-frequentist-platform-multi-arm-multi-stage-designs] Each new arm was a substantial amendment needing ethics and regulatory review, and drug supply could set the pace. [20/governance-firewalls-and-the-monitoring-board]

## Pitfalls

An arm opened after a change in standard of care would have a different control population from one opened before it. [20/the-frequentist-platform-multi-arm-multi-stage-designs]

## Check yourself

- Q: How was control allocated at the start? A: 2:1:1:1:1:1, twice the share of each research arm. [20/the-frequentist-platform-multi-arm-multi-stage-designs]
- Q: How were new arms added? A: By substantial amendment. [20/the-frequentist-platform-multi-arm-multi-stage-designs]
