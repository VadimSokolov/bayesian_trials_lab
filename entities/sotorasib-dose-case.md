## What it is
Sotorasib is a KRAS G12C inhibitor for non-small-cell lung cancer. It is the case most often cited for Project Optimus. [17/sotorasib-and-the-dose-that-was-not-optimized]

## Why it matters
This is the situation the August 2024 guidance was written for: a dose chosen without a randomized comparison of lower doses, with evidence for a lower dose collected only after approval. [17/sotorasib-and-the-dose-that-was-not-optimized] The cost is then borne by patients and a postmarketing study, rather than by a randomized comparison in phase 1/2. [17/sotorasib-and-the-dose-that-was-not-optimized]

## In the book
The pivotal single-arm trial used the 960 mg dose. [17/sotorasib-and-the-dose-that-was-not-optimized] The accelerated approval of May 2021 came with a postmarketing requirement to compare that dose with a lower one. [17/sotorasib-and-the-dose-that-was-not-optimized] The randomized comparison of 960 mg and 240 mg was run only after approval. [17/sotorasib-and-the-dose-that-was-not-optimized]
