## What it is

GBM AGILE (NCT03970447) is a Bayesian response-adaptive platform in glioblastoma designed with registration in mind, which I-SPY 2 was not. [20/gbm-agile-a-registration-platform] It uses a seamless phase 2/3 structure. In stage 1 each arm is randomized adaptively against a shared control (temozolomide in newly diagnosed disease, lomustine in recurrent), with allocation proportional to the posterior probability of a hazard ratio below a benchmark and arms evaluated monthly. An arm that clears the bar graduates to stage 2 with fixed randomization to confirm the finding. [20/gbm-agile-a-registration-platform]

## Why it matters

Patients are classified into signatures by newly diagnosed versus recurrent disease and MGMT methylation status, and 20% of each subtype's patients are assigned to control. [20/gbm-agile-a-registration-platform] FDA's Bayesian draft names the platform as its example for the time machine, since the shared control must be used carefully when standard of care changes. [20/gbm-agile-a-registration-platform]

## In the book

For regorafenib, efficacy required a Bayesian probability of benefit (hazard ratio below 1) of at least 98%, and enrollment stopped for limited efficacy when predictive power fell below 25% in all signatures. The report has since been corrected by an erratum. [20/gbm-agile-a-registration-platform] The 98% is set by simulation so that the false positive rate is acceptable, not by a view about the prior. [20/gbm-agile-a-registration-platform] The registry lists 17 arm entries, several running dose-finding and enhanced safety management phases first, which is a pattern worth copying. [20/gbm-agile-a-registration-platform]

## Pitfalls

Thresholds like 98% are calibrated per design, so they cannot be carried over to another platform without a new simulation report. [20/gbm-agile-a-registration-platform]

## Check yourself

- Q: What does a stage 1 graduate do next? A: Moves to stage 2 with fixed randomization. [20/gbm-agile-a-registration-platform]
- Q: What share of each subtype is on control? A: 20%. [20/gbm-agile-a-registration-platform]
