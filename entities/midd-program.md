## What it is
The model-informed drug development paired meeting program offers paired meetings with FDA on modeling and simulation in a specific development program. [3/the-midd-paired-meeting-program] Examples are dose selection, trial design simulation, and an exposure-response model supporting a labeling claim. [3/the-midd-paired-meeting-program]

## Why it matters
It is the procedural counterpart of ICH M15. [3/the-midd-paired-meeting-program] M15 sets principles for assessing model credibility, and the program is where a sponsor tests a particular modeling plan against FDA's reading. [3/the-midd-paired-meeting-program] M15 defines model-informed drug development as the use of computational modeling and simulation that can integrate nonclinical data, clinical data and prior information. [sec-regulatory-ich]

## In the book
A Bayesian adaptive design with a model-based dose-response component can fall under both the CID and MIDD programs. [3/the-midd-paired-meeting-program] The book's advice is to ask which fits the question. [3/the-midd-paired-meeting-program] A dose that depends on a model points to the MIDD program. [3/how-to-choose-the-meeting]

## Check yourself
- Q: Which program handles the models that feed a design's decision rules? A: The MIDD paired meeting program. [3/the-midd-paired-meeting-program]
- Q: Is the outcome binding? A: No. [3/how-to-choose-the-meeting]
