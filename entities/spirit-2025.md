## What it is
SPIRIT 2025 is a checklist of minimum items a protocol of a randomized trial should address. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
SPIRIT 2013 is its antecedent. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## Why it matters
Two standards shape the protocol document itself, ICH M11 and SPIRIT 2025, and neither is specific to Bayesian designs. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
The chapter's regulatory anchor lists both under how to ask, when, and in what format. [sec-protocol]

## In the book
The ICH M11 template does not name the Bayesian components (prior, simulation-based calibration, predictive probability rules), so the sponsor places them where a reviewer would look for the traditional counterpart. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
The sponsor checklist asks that the protocol be mapped into the M11 template and the SPIRIT items. [23/checklists]
The items a Bayesian trial adds are the prior and its source, the success criterion with its threshold and calibration, the interim schedule and the simulation report, the software and seeds, and the sensitivity analyses to the prior. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

## Pitfalls
None of the reporting guidelines around the protocol is a regulatory requirement for a Bayesian trial. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]

- Q: Is SPIRIT 2025 a regulatory requirement for Bayesian trials? A: No; the book says none of the reporting guidelines around the protocol is a regulatory requirement, and SPIRIT 2025 is not Bayesian-specific. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
- Q: What does SPIRIT 2025 list? A: Minimum items a randomized-trial protocol should address. [23/structured-protocols-ich-m11-and-spirit-2025]
