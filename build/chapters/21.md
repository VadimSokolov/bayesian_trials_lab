## Summary
This chapter reconstructs landmark trials to show what a Bayesian design would have changed and what it would have cost. SELECT (semaglutide and cardiovascular events) is the main case: the trial as conducted, three monitoring schemes and SELECT's own design with one error rate, the saving in calendar time and exposure, what the monitoring committee would have seen, and what a committee and a regulator would have had to accept. REWIND (dulaglutide) is the case where the scheduled design wins, with a Bayesian reconstruction and the final data under skeptical priors. SURPASS CVOT is an active comparator with a noninferiority margin, TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 a prespecified biomarker strategy with baseline tau strata, and BNT162b2 a Bayesian confirmatory trial that led to an authorization. The chapter values the saving (events and quality adjusted life years, trial cost, timeline and the acceleration bound), compares the protocol side by side and states the limits of reconstruction.
