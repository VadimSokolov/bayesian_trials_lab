## Summary
This appendix gives the regulatory documents cited in the book with their status as of November 2026, the products and trials named in FDA's January 2026 Bayesian draft guidance, and the FDA programs a sponsor can use. The documents include the FDA draft on Bayesian methodology, ICH E20 on adaptive designs, the FDA guidances on adaptive designs, master protocols, substantial evidence, multiple endpoints, optimizing dosage, data monitoring committees, externally controlled trials and device statistics, ICH E8(R1), E9, E9(R1), E10, E11A, E4, M11 and M15, FDA guidances on artificial intelligence and new approach methodologies, formal meetings and special protocol assessment, the diabetes cardiovascular risk guidance, the EMA and HMA question and answer document on complex trials, the EMA reflection paper on platform trials and the MHRA draft on external control arms. The programs are the Complex Innovative Trial Design and MIDD paired meeting programs, Critical Path Innovation Meetings and Drug Development Tool qualification. It contains no results.
