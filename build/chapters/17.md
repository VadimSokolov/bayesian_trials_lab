## Summary
This chapter asks which dose to carry forward. It starts with the traditional 3+3 design, its operating characteristics by simulation and what six patients can tell you. It then gives model based and model assisted designs: the continual reassessment method, the Bayesian optimal interval design (BOIN), mTPI, mTPI 2 and Keyboard, a comparison, escalation with overdose control, a worked logistic model decision and protocol language. It goes beyond the maximum tolerated dose to efficacy, toxicity and dose optimization: why the MTD is a poor target for modern agents, what the FDA guidance asks, a utility formulation, EffTox and phase 1/2 designs, late onset toxicity and time to event interval designs, and randomized dose comparison. It covers dose response estimation outside oncology by MCP Mod, adaptive enrichment, and seamless phase 2/3 designs. Cases: use of model based designs, BOIN and FDA's fit for purpose determination, sotorasib, dulaglutide AWARD 5, lecanemab phase 2b and ASTIN.
