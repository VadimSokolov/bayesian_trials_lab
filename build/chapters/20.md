## Summary
This chapter asks how one trial infrastructure can answer many questions. It starts with the cost of separate trials, defines master protocol, basket, umbrella and platform, and describes frequentist multi arm multi stage designs. It does the shared control arithmetic (including the number of false positives), then concurrent and non concurrent controls: the regulatory position, a simulation of the bias, and the Bayesian time machine. It treats multiplicity across arms (FDA, EMA and Bayesian error control) and Bayesian platforms: I-SPY 2 graduation by predictive probability, GBM AGILE as a registration platform, REMAP-CAP domains and response adaptive randomization, the COVID anticoagulation multiplatform and the pragmatic pandemic platforms RECOVERY and PANORAMIC. It compares traditional and Bayesian platforms, covers governance, firewalls and the monitoring board, EPAD (a platform that never opened an arm), and what changes in the protocol.
