## Summary
This chapter asks what a Bayesian protocol must say and how to work with regulators. It goes through the protocol section by section, traditional first and then what changes: the prior, the success criterion and its calibration, the interim schedule and decision rules, adaptive randomization, safety rules and the estimand. It covers the statistical analysis plan and the simulation report, structured protocols (ICH M11 and SPIRIT 2025), reporting guidelines (SPIRIT, CONSORT 2025, the adaptive designs extension, guidance on statistical analysis plans and the Bayesian checklists ROBUST and BARG), the data monitoring committee and firewalls, and the interaction timeline with FDA (including Special Protocol Assessment). It covers EMA and other regulators and the substantial evidence question of two trials, one trial, and where Bayesian evidence fits. Cases: BNT162b2, DAWN, REBYOTA, pediatric belimumab and the named examples in the January 2026 draft. It states what the Bayesian design does not buy, gives checklists and open questions.
