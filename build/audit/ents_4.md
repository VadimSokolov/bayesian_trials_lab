MINOR confounding-by-indication:Why it matters | "The coin balances health as recorded, as sensed and as nobody has thought of." | A coin balances these only on average (in expectation); any single 400-patient trial still has chance imbalance. MINOR two-group-comparison:Pitfalls | "Randomization lets the observed gap stand in for the unobservable average difference." | Same simplification: it makes the gap an unbiased estimate, not equal to the average effect | "Randomization makes the observed gap an unbiased estimate of the unobservable average difference, with chance error measured by the standard error."
