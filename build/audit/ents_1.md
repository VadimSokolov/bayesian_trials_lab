MINOR hazard-ratio:15 (Check yourself) | "Is a hazard ratio of 0.8 a 20 percent hazard reduction? A: Yes." | Correct only as a relative reduction in the instantaneous rate. Readers routinely read it as 20% fewer events or a 20% lower risk at a fixed time, which is wrong (the risk ratio at time t differs, and the absolute reduction depends on the baseline rate). | "A: Yes, a 20 percent relative reduction in the hazard; it is not a 20 percent reduction in the risk of the event by a given time, and it says nothing about the absolute reduction."
