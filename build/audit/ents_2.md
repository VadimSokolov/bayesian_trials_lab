MAJOR type-i-error:summary and What it is | "Rejecting the null when the drug has no effect, which means approving something useless" / "approving something useless" | A type I error is a false-positive trial conclusion (rejecting H0 when it is true). It is not an approval: approval rests on substantial evidence (usually more than one trial, or one trial plus confirmatory evidence) and a benefit-risk judgment. The brief names this exact conflation | Summary: "Rejecting the null when the drug has no effect, a false-positive trial result; its probability is the level alpha of the test." Body: "A type I error is rejecting $H_0$ when the drug has no effect: a false-positive trial, which could lead to approval of an ineffective drug if nothing else catches it."
