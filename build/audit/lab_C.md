## Summary
2 MAJOR, 30 MINOR across modules 09 to 13. Named-trial facts mostly check out against the book and the literature: AWARD-5, lecanemab Study 201 (33%/27%, 0.64 vs 0.80, 48%/12% shares), ECMO 11/11 vs 0/1, STAMPEDE, CAST, WHI 1.29/1.41, thalidomide, the meeting timelines.
Main problems: CALGB 49907's stopping rule is misstated against the book (09); "false approvals" for type I error (13); "safety" used to mean protection against false wins (12); claims that are too absolute (single-arm studies, adapting "before the first patient", designs "agreed" at meetings); one-sided vs two-sided labels mixed within 11.
Unverifiable 2026 guidance dates (master protocols, substantial evidence, formal meetings, M15, NAMs) need a check against the cited FDA and ICH URLs. Population wording is fine apart from Phase II/III "people" in 13.
