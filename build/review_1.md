## Overall assessment

Pages with no findings: ddt-qualification, noninferiority-margin, noninferiority-trial, intercurrent-event, arnold-randomness-measure, skeptical-prior (6 of 26). Most of the rest have only edge or polish issues. Numbers, dates, trial results and regulatory statuses check out against the rendered sections almost everywhere: SELECT, SURPASS-CVOT, RECOVERY, REBYOTA thresholds, ILLUMINATE, FPR table values, assurance table, skeptical-prior table (508 events, 131 ESS, 0.988, 0.84), Arnold values, and eq-lifecycle-cost, eq-ni-hyp, eq-arnold, eq-definetti, eq-predictive, eq-fpr, eq-ockham, eq-assurance and eq-assurance-normal are all copied exactly. Common patterns: (1) `illustrates` edges written in the wrong direction from the concept page to a trial (clinical-trial, two-group-comparison, one-trial, asa-statement), and `governed-by` reversed on a regulation page; (2) edges that repeat a prerequisite, against SCHEMA (real-options, one-trial, mrc, de-finetti, assurance, fda-multiple-endpoints); (3) loose concept-to-concept relations (`illustrates`, `special-case-of`, `generalizes`) where no relation, or `uses`, is right; (4) "Pitfalls" sections that hold restated facts rather than pitfalls; (5) "In the book" sections that skip the section's key numbers (table values) the reader would want; (6) a few unsupported or mis-keyed sentences (real-options summary, asa-statement key) and one inherited book slip on the sign of a log hazard ratio (sec-priors-conflict).
