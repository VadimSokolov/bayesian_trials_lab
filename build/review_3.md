## Overall assessment

26 pages reviewed: 2 MAJOR, 31 MINOR (one of them a skeleton note). No invented numbers, dates, trial results or regulatory statuses were found; every figure checked (SELECT, BNT162b2, EOLIA, PANORAMIC, belimumab, EPAD, power-prior and hierarchical examples, Project Optimus scope) matches the rendered sections, and all LaTeX (eq-ped-mix, eq-hier, eq-shrink, eq-power, eq-npp, eq-borrow-marg) is copied correctly. Pages that are clean or close to clean: hierarchical-model, epad-trial, gsdesign, select-trial (apart from one edge), belimumab-pediatric-lupus (apart from its edges).

Common patterns: (1) related edges point the wrong way, especially `illustrates` and `used-in` written from the concept or method side, and `precedes` misused; (2) prerequisites repeated in related, against SCHEMA (about 10 pages); (3) "In the book" sections that leave out the section's headline numbers (efftox, overdose-control, cure-fraction, gamma-posterior-hazard, weighted-log-rank-test, pediatric-extrapolation, label-revenue), so the pages are accurate but thin for a clinician; (4) "What it is" sometimes describes the example rather than defining the entity (vaccine-efficacy-trial, pediatric-extrapolation); (5) governed-by pointing at organizations (fda, ema); (6) the software pages fall under the 150-word floor because the book's own treatment is one line each.
