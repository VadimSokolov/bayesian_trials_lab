Bayesian Clinical Trials

Alexandra Sokolova, Vadim Sokolov and Nicholas G. Polson (2026)

This book is for the people who run clinical trials: investigators, physicians, biostatisticians, data monitoring committee members and regulatory reviewers. It starts from the traditional trial, shows the Bayesian version of the same decision on the same numbers, and says plainly where the traditional design is the better choice.
