Talking to FDA about a new design

FDA's Complex Innovative Trial Design (CID) paired meeting program is where sponsors discuss designs such as Bayesian ones with FDA staff.

Designs are usually discussed at an end-of-phase-2 meeting (Type B) or a Type C meeting; binding agreement needs a Special Protocol Assessment. The clocks above apply.

Regulator's corner

FDA says a Type A meeting is for a program that is stuck:

"necessary for an otherwise stalled drug or biological product development program to proceed or to address an important safety issue."

Section III. FDA, Formal Meetings Between the FDA and Sponsors or Applicants of PDUFA Products (final, August 2026).

Takeaway

  • The road has stages, and FDA checks in at each big step.
  • Pick the meeting that fits the question: stuck, early, or small.
  • The sponsor oversees the trial, even when a CRO does the work. The CRO answers for the duties it took over in writing.
